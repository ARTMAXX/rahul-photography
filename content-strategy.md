## Content Formats

1. **Blog articles** — Long-form (1,500-2,500 words), SEO-optimized
2. **Case study pages** — Before/after shoots with ROI metrics
3. **Service explainers** — What to expect during a product photoshoot
4. **Behind-the-scenes** — Instagram/TikTok video content repurposed to blog

---

## Implementation Priority

1. Create blog route (`/blog`) — Next.js app router
2. Publish 2 articles to start: one location-specific, one service-specific
3. Add blog links from services section on homepage
4. Monthly cadence: 2 articles/month for first 3 months, then 1/month

---

## AI Search Optimization Notes

- Each blog post should include a clear "tl;dr" summary for AI extraction
- Use FAQ schema on each article targeting the primary question
- Include pricing context when relevant (AI searches increasingly look for cost data)
- Link internally from blog to the main site sections
