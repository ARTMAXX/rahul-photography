# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners, brand managers, marketing directors, and creative leads — primarily in India — who need high-end commercial product, food, beverage, and fashion photography. They are discerning, value-driven buyers who judge quality by the craft of the work itself. Their context: evaluating a photographer's portfolio to decide whether to invest ₹20,000–₹2,00,000+ in a brand shoot. They land on this page with high expectations; the site must meet them before they scroll.

## Product Purpose

Convert high-value commercial photography prospects into booked clients by showcasing Rahul Chanda's craft through a cinematic, editorial-quality portfolio experience. Success is a lead reaching out via the contact form or WhatsApp — and feeling confident before they do. The site itself must demonstrate the same visual standards Rahul sells: precision lighting, intentional composition, restrained elegance.

## Brand Personality

**Cinematic. Precision. Luxe.**

- **Cinematic** — dark, atmospheric, scroll-driven narrative pacing. Every section transitions like a film scene.
- **Precision** — intentional spacing, exacting typography, sharp focus on craft. No slop, no half-measures.
- **Luxe** — restrained wealth of quality. Gold accents, deep blacks, rich textures. Understated confidence, not flashy.

Voice: confident but not arrogant. Editorial, not "sales page." Speaks to peers who recognise craft when they see it.

## Anti-references

Avoid all of the following:

- **Generic AI-generated templates** — the warm-neutral body-bg SaaS look, 2023-era uppercase kicker eyebrows above every section, numbered 01/02/03 scaffolding, identical card grids with icon+heading+text.
- **Loud or cluttered** — no chaotic compositions, overlaid text-on-text collisions, or noisy gradients that compete with the photography.
- **Overly minimalist white-space-heavy portfolios** — the "tons of breathing room, tiny text, no visual weight" school. The work needs presence; the page should frame it, not disappear.
- **SaaS-cream aesthetic** — no beige/sand/cream backgrounds, no glassmorphism-as-default, no gradient text.
- **"Tech startup" dark mode** — flat, cheap-looking dark UIs with no depth or texture. The dark here should feel cinematic and rich, not utilitarian.

## Design Principles

1. **The work is the hero.** Every layout decision exists to frame the photography. Typography, motion, and spacing serve the images, not compete with them.
2. **Cinematic rhythm.** Scroll should feel like moving through a film — deliberate pacing, scene transitions, reveals that earn their timing. No uniform stagger; each section gets the entrance it deserves.
3. **Restrained confidence.** Luxe is what you leave out. Fewer elements, better executed. One gold accent, not three. Intentional whitespace, not filler.
4. **Precision in every detail.** Kerning, leading, alignment, animation curves — nothing is accidental. The site signals the same meticulousness Rahul brings to a shoot.
5. **Usability at the edges.** Ultra high-end visual experience doesn't mean broken UX. All buttons are clickable (custom cursor respects interactive elements), forms submit, navigation is intuitive, the site works on mobile. Craft includes the invisible parts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Ultra high-end visual experience with user-friendly usability. Target reasonable accessibility:

- Text contrast where readable (large headings may break strict AA for cinematic effect)
- All interactive elements are reachable and clickable (custom cursor has pointer states)
- Keyboard navigation works for menus and forms
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected — animations degrade gracefully, no content gated on transitions
- Forms are functional with proper labels, validation, and backend submission
- Semantic HTML structure for screen reader context
