## Process

1. **Discovery & Brief** — Deep dive into brand, product, and vision
2. **Concept & Moodboard** — Visual direction proposal with references and lighting diagrams
3. **Production Day** — Controlled studio environment with on-set previews
4. **Editing & Delivery** — Color grading, retouching, multiple format delivery
