## 5. GBP REVIEW SHORTLINK — How to Get It

1. Open **Google Business Profile Manager** (web or app)
2. Click **"Get more reviews"** (blue button, top right)
3. Copy the **short URL** (looks like `https://g.page/r/.../review` or `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?...`)
4. Save it — this is your permanent review link
5. Test it in incognito → should open "Write a review" overlay

**Pro tip:** Add UTM for tracking:
```
https://g.page/r/XXXXXX/review?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=post-delivery&utm_campaign=review-request
```

## 8. WHAT NOT TO SAY (Policy Violations)

| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Instead |
|--------------|------------|
| "Give me a 5-star review" | "An honest review about your experience" |
| "Mention 'best photographer Dehradun'" | "Mention the type of project we did" |
| "I'll give you a discount for a review" | (Never incentivize) |
| "My cousin needs a review" | (Never ask family/friends) |
| "Copy this text: 'Best photographer...'" | "In your own words..." |
