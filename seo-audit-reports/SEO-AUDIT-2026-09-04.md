## 1. Crawlability & Indexation — PASS with 1 fix

### What's right
- **robots.txt** (live): allows all crawlers, sitemap + host declared, AI-crawler friendly (Content-Signal policy documented in `src/app/robots.ts`).
- **Sitemap** (live): 34 URLs — 10 core pages, 4 service pages, 17 blog posts (5 standalone + 12 dynamic). Real hand-maintained `lastmod` dates (Aug 2026), correct priority hierarchy. No orphaned URLs: `src/app/sitemap.ts` derives entries from `postDates` + standalone slugs, matching `generateStaticParams`.
- **IndexNow** integrated (`scripts/setup-indexnow.js`, `/api/indexnow`, `[key]` route) — instant indexing pings for Bing/Yandex.
- **Google site verification** present in root metadata; GA4, Clarity, Ahrefs Web Analytics installed.
- AI-accessibility extras: `Accept: text/markdown` support + WebMCP tools registered for AI agents (`search_portfolio`, `get_services`, `get_page_markdown`) — good AEO/GEO play.

### Issue: `Disallow: /_next/` too broad
- **Evidence (live robots.txt):** `Disallow: /_next/` blocks `/_next/static/**` — the CSS/JS Googlebot needs to render the page.
- **Impact:** Medium. Content is SSR'd so the HTML survives, but Google's renderer gets "Page fetch" blocks, which can degrade mobile-friendliness evaluation and JS-dependent content (gallery filters, GSAP-driven sections).
- **Fix:** in `src/app/robots.ts` change to:
  ```ts
  disallow: ["/api/", "/_next/data/"],
  ```

## 3. Structured Data — PASS with 1 risk

Present: `WebSite` + `Organization/LocalBusiness` + `Person` graph, `BreadcrumbList`, `BlogPosting` (with dates, author, image), `FAQPage`, `AggregateRating`.

### Issue: standalone `AggregateRating` (hardcoded 4.9)
- **Evidence:** `generateReviewSchema()` in `src/lib/schemas.ts` emits a bare `AggregateRating` with `ratingValue: "4.9"` — no `itemReviewed`, no `Review` objects.
- **Impact:** Medium. Google requires ratings attached to a specific entity, and **self-serving reviews on a LocalBusiness are ineligible for rich results**. Risks being ignored at best, a structured-data manual action at worst.
- **Fix:** either (a) remove it, or (b) nest real `Review` objects (named authors matching the visible testimonials) inside the `LocalBusiness` node and keep `aggregateRating` there.
