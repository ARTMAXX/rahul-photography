# Competitor Profile: CK Studio (ckstudio.in)

## Overview
- **URL**: https://ckstudio.in/
- **Platform**: Custom (appears PHP/WordPress-like)
- **Location**: Delhi NCR (also services Dehradun, Mussoorie, Rishikesh, Haridwar)
- **Niche**: E-commerce product photography at scale
- **Established**: 8+ years (founder Prince)
- **Team Size**: 10+ years combined experience

## Positioning & Messaging
- **Tagline**: "Elevate your ecommerce business with stunning product photography"
- **Target Market**: E-commerce sellers (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.)
- **Tone**: Practical, direct, price-conscious
- **Unique Diff**: Platform-specific expertise — knows marketplace compliance requirements

## Services
- Product Photography (white background, ghost mannequin, lifestyle, flat lay)
- Fashion Photography
- Lookbook Photography
- 360° Product Photography
- Ecommerce Video
- FMCG Photography
- Mobile Accessories, Footwear, Handbag, Backpack, Lingerie
- Ghost Mannequin
- Model Photography (male, female — Myntra-approved)
- Image Editing & Retouching
- Infographic Creation

- Ghost Mannequin: ₹250/image
- Lifestyle: ₹275/image
- 360° Product: ₹100/frame (min 24)
- Flat Lay: ₹200/image

## Equipment
- Sony A7R5 full frame
- Godox digital lighting
- Myntra-approved models, makeup artists, stylists, props

## Platform Expertise
Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Meesho, eBay, Paytm, Snapdeal, Tata Cliq, Shopclues, Jabong

## Portfolio Categories (35+)
E-Commerce, Jewellery, Fashion, Backpack, Lingerie, Ghost Mannequin, Footwear, Cosmetic, Ecommerce Video, Garment, 360°, Male Model, Watch, Flipkart, Lookbook, Hand Bag, Automotive, Furniture, Infographic, Lifestyle, Flat Lay, Sanitaryware, Handicraft, Electronic, Mobile Accessories, Kitchen Wear, Industrial, Medicine, Commercial, FMCG, Home & Furnishing

## Strengths
- **Pricing transparency** — huge trust advantage, especially for price-conscious e-commerce sellers
- Extremely broad category coverage (35+ segments)
- Platform-specific compliance knowledge (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra)
- Equipment transparency builds trust
- Telugu/Hindi bilingual (Hindi homepage heading)
- Physical studio in Delhi with model/wardrobe/styling resources
- Specific equipment claims (Sony A7R5, Godox)

## Weaknesses
- Extremely weak brand — no personal story or differentiation beyond "we do photography"
- Low-quality visual presentation — portfolio images not impressive
- Website design is outdated and cluttered
- No blog, no content marketing
- No individual photographer branding (Prince is named but not profiled)
- "Factory" positioning (volumes/pricing) — hard to raise prices
- No testimonials or social proof from named clients
- No social media presence visible
- Pricing transparency is both a strength and a ceiling — hard to move upmarket

## Key Takeaway for Rahul Chanda
CK Studio competes on **price and volume** — the opposite of Rahul's premium positioning. They're a threat only for budget-conscious e-commerce clients. Rahul should NOT compete on price but can use CK Studio as a "this is what cheap looks like" contrast in his positioning. The fact that CK Studio serves Dehradun (same city) is notable — but they're serving a different market segment entirely.
