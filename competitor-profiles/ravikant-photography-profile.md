## Key Takeaway for Rahul Chanda
Ravikant competes through **breadth** — covering many niches with dedicated pages. His SEO strategy targets long-tail keywords via sub-pages. However, the site lacks brand differentiation, content authority, and a compelling personal narrative. Rahul can outperform by combining niche coverage with a strong personal brand (E-E-A-T), better site architecture, and content marketing.
