Real Condensation (Best)

Chill the glass in freezer 2-3 hours. Place in room-temperature environment. Condensation forms naturally over 5-10 minutes. Shoot quickly before droplets evaporate.

Water Spray Method

Spray cold water on glass with fine mister. Creates immediate condensation effect. Lasts 20-30 minutes before evaporating.

Glycerin Drops (Professional)

Place glycerin drops on glass. They don't evaporate. Mix glycerin with water for realistic droplet effect. Removes easily in post-production.
