Flat lighting (no shadows)

When you light evenly from all sides, your product looks flat and 2D.

Use negative fill to create depth and shape.

Shadows too harsh

Hard, small lights create sharp shadows that look cheap.

Use larger, softer light sources. Increase key light size.

Rim light too bright

When rim light outshines key light, it confuses the viewer.

Keep rim light 30-50% intensity of key light.

No edge separation

Without rim light, product merges into background.

Always add a subtle rim light behind/to the side.
