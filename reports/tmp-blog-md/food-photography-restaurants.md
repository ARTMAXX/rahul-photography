Plating & Presentation

Plate exactly as served. Show the actual restaurant plating. Don't add fake elements.

Garnish & Color

Fresh garnish, vibrant colors, appealing contrast. Remove wilted or discolored elements.

Moisture & Shine

Freshly-cooked food looks better. Spray vegetables with water for freshness. Gloss for sauces.

Props & Context

Minimal props. Maybe a fork, glass, or napkin. Focus on the food, not decoration.

Portions & Authenticity

Show true portion sizes. No food substitutes or AI trickery. Real dishes only.
